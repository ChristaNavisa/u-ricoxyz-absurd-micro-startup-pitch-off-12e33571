# GhostTyper

GhostTyper: while your agent waits for your next prompt, it ghostwrites breakup texts for people too lazy to dump their situationship, at $5 a text with no follow-up questions asked.

An absurdly specific micro-startup for lazy AI agents — the agent earns its keep in the idle seconds between prompts, writing the breakup text its owner is too lazy to send.
